How to Use

                
A quick guide to hiding and extracting data securely.

            

            

                

                    
1

                    

                        
Hide Data in an Image

                        
Go to Hide Data. Select a PNG carrier image, type a message or pick a file, set an optional password, then tap Encrypt & Embed to download your secured PNG.

                    

                

                

                    
2

                    

                        
Share the Image

                        
The image looks identical to the original. Share via email, Discord, Slack, or Drive. Never use WhatsApp or Twitter — they compress images and destroy hidden data.

                    

                

                

                    
3

                    

                        
Extract the Secret

                        
Open Extract Data, upload the PNG, enter the password (if any), and tap Extract Payload to recover your data instantly.

                    

                

                

                    

                        
                        Important Warning
                    

                    
If the image is resized, compressed, or converted to JPEG, hidden data is permanently destroyed. Always use lossless file transfer methods.

                

            

        


        
        

            

                
            

            
AES-256 + LSB

            
All processing is 100% local. Encrypted via Web Crypto API, distributed via Fisher-Yates PRNG. Nothing ever leaves your device.
