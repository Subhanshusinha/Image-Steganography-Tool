How to Use

                
A quick guide to hiding and extracting data securely.

            

            

                
                

                    
1

                    

                        
Hide Data in an Image

                        
Go to the Hide Data tab. Drop any PNG image into the carrier box, then type a message or upload a file. Optionally set a password, then click Encrypt & Embed to download the secured image.

                    

                

                
                

                    
2

                    

                        
Share the Image

                        
The image looks identical to the original. Send it via email, Discord, Slack, or Google Drive. Do not use WhatsApp or Twitter as they compress images and destroy the hidden data.

                    

                

                
                

                    
3

                    

                        
Extract the Secret

                        
Open the Extract Data tab. Drop the secured PNG, enter the password (if any), and click Extract Payload to recover your data.

                    

                

                
                

                    

                        
                        Important Warning
                    

                    
If the image is resized, compressed, or converted (e.g. to JPEG), the hidden payload is permanently destroyed. Always share via lossless methods.

                

            

        


        
        

            

                
            

            
AES-256 + LSB Steganography

            
All data stays in your browser. Encrypted via Web Crypto API, distributed via Fisher-Yates PRNG. No data ever leaves your device.
