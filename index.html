How to Use StealthGuard

                    
A quick guide to hiding and extracting files securely.

                

                
                

                    
                    
                    

                        
1

                        

                            
Hide Data in an Image

                            
Go to the Hide Data tab. Drag and drop any normal PNG image into the Cover Image box. This image will be the carrier.

                            
Next, type a secret message or upload a file (like a PDF or ZIP). Optionally, enter a strong password. Click Encrypt & Embed to generate your new, secured PNG image.

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
2

                        

                            
Share the Image

                            
The downloaded image looks completely identical to the original to the human eye. You can send this PNG image to anyone via email, USB, or uncompressed messaging apps (ensure the app doesn't re-compress images like WhatsApp does, or send it as a document).

                        

                    


                    
                    

                        
3

                        

                            
Extract the Secret

                            
To recover the hidden data, the recipient must open StealthGuard and go to the Extract Data tab. They simply drop the secured PNG image into the box, enter the password (if you set one), and click Extract Payload.

                        

                    


                    

                        

                            
                            Important Warning
                        

                        
Steganography relies on exact pixel values. If the image is resized, compressed, filtered, or converted to JPEG by a social media site, the hidden data will be permanently destroyed. Always share the image via lossless methods (Email as attachment, ZIP, Slack, Discord, Google Drive, etc).
